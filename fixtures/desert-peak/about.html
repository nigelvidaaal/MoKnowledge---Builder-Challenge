Our Story

Desert Peak Plumbing was founded in 1998 by Maria Torres, a third-generation plumber who started with one truck and a promise to treat every customer like family. Today our team of 14 licensed technicians serves thousands of homes across the valley.

We believe great service starts with honesty. That's why we give you a flat-rate price before any work begins, and we never charge for overtime on emergencies.

Every spring we donate free water heater flushes to seniors through the Henderson Community Center, and our team volunteers with Habitat for Humanity Las Vegas.
